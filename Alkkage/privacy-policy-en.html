Privacy Policy

Alkkagi Master (알까기 명인)
Effective: November 19, 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what information the mobile game Alkkagi Master (the "App"), operated by MSOON (the "Operator"), handles and how it is used and protected.

In short: there is no sign-up, and the App never asks for your name, phone number, email or precise location. For online matches and the weekly ranking it sends only a random per-device ID, the nickname you choose, and game records. Your saved game is kept on our server briefly only when you use device transfer. A highlight scene and its results are stored on our server only when you send it to a friend, try a challenge, or submit it for Shot of the Week.

1. Stored only on your device

  • Stage records, stars, attendance points, cosmetics and titles
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language, theme, controls, effects)
  • Your nickname and your consent choices
  • Highlights (saved replay scenes: stone positions, shot, theme, date), received scenes and challenge records

This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Ads and analytics

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) may process your advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from your IP address to show ads. In the EEA and similar regions, Google's consent form asks you first, and you can change it in Settings › Ad privacy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) processes anonymous usage statistics only if you allow analytics.

See the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

4. Consent and withdrawal

  • Agreeing to the Terms of Service and this policy is required to use the App.
  • Analytics is optional. You choose on first launch and can turn it off any time in Settings › More › Usage analytics. Once off, no further statistics are sent.
  • Anonymous error reports cannot identify you and may be sent regardless of this choice to keep the App stable.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder for the daily challenge, it is scheduled on your device. Nothing about it is sent to our server. You can turn it off in the App or device settings.

6. Retention

  • Online match data: deleted automatically 3 days after the last move.
  • Weekly ranking records (nickname, score, shot replays): kept while the service runs, for ranking and anti-cheat review; deleted promptly on request.
  • Anonymous usage statistics and error reports: kept while the service runs; deleted promptly on request.
  • Theme ideas: kept while the service runs, to make and refine themes, and deleted promptly on request. Only the Operator reads them; other users never see your text. If your idea becomes a theme, your nickname appears in that theme's guide only if you turned on name display.
  • Device transfer bundle: deleted as soon as the new device receives it. An unused code stops working after 7 days and is then deleted from our server.
  • Highlights sent to friends and their challenge results: automatically deleted from our server 90 days after first sent. Scenes picked as Shot of the Week and their rankings are kept while the service runs, to reuse past scenes and check rankings. Either way, deleted promptly on request.
  • On-device data: until you uninstall the App.

7. Sharing and international transfer

We do not sell or hand over your data. Apart from the Google services in section 3, nothing is shared. Your nickname is visible only to opponents in matches you start and on the weekly ranking. A highlight you send can be viewed by anyone who has its code, and your nickname appears with it and with your challenge results only if you choose to include it. If your scene is picked as Shot of the Week, the scene and your nickname are shown to all users for that week, and the Shot of the Week ranking shows challengers' nicknames and tries.

Our server and the ads and analytics services the App uses are run by Google LLC, so your data may be stored and processed in Google data centers outside Korea.

8. Your rights and deletion requests

  • Uninstalling the App deletes all on-device data. Create a device transfer code first if you want to move your progress.
  • To delete or correct your nickname or ranking records on our server, email us your nickname and the week(s) you played.
  • To delete a highlight you sent or your challenge results, email us the 6-letter code of that scene (or the device ID shown in the app settings). Reports of inappropriate scenes go to the same email.
  • Report inappropriate nicknames to the same address.
  • To delete a theme idea you sent, email us with the device ID shown in the app settings. Please do not put contact details or other personal information in an idea; links, email addresses and phone numbers are removed by our server before saving.

9. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and will delete it if we learn of it.

10. Security

  • No accounts; only an anonymous ID
  • HTTPS for all traffic; server access limited to the Operator
  • Word filter for nicknames and theme ideas
